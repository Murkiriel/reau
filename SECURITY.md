# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: on this
repository, open the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Describe what you found, the app version
and the steps to reproduce it.

You will get an answer as soon as the author can look at it. This is a spare-time project: there is no support
contract and no fixed response time.

## Before you attach anything to a report

Logs, recordings and photos may contain places you went, Wi-Fi names, phone numbers or names from your contacts. Look
through them first and cut out what you do not want public. When in doubt, describe it in words instead.
