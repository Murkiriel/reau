Privacy and terms

What leaves the phone

To the dash, over its Wi-Fi: the picture and, with the switches on, the music, the caller and the phone status. The only trip to the internet is the weather on the dash (Open-Meteo), optional and off by default. With the (optional) network server on, what leaves also reaches devices on your local network.

What stays on the phone

A diagnostic log (on by default), without position, streets, songs or caller; the dash’s Wi-Fi name and password and what the dash reported, encrypted with an Android key; and, if you turn them on, the rounded position (about 11 km), the last weather and the debug recordings. Everything stays in the app’s internal folder and only leaves through Share.

What you control

In Settings, Debug: turn the log off, view it, delete logs and recordings and choose after how many days they delete themselves (default: 30 days). The “REau and your data” screen, in the app itself, describes everything.

Out of REau’s reach

What reaches Android Auto belongs to it: your voice in Gemini and Waze routes follow Google’s and Waze’s rules.

How to report a problem

Open an issue in the repository with the app version, the phone and what happened. Before attaching logs, recordings or photos, look at the file and cut out what you don’t want public: they may contain places you went, Wi-Fi names, phone numbers or contact names. Security flaws go through the private vulnerability report (Security tab), never a public issue.
